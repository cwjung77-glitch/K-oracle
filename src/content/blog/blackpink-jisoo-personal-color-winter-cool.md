## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

BLACKPINK's Jisoo is the textbook definition of a **True Winter Cool (겨울 쿨톤 트루)**. She possesses a high-contrast beauty: striking dark hair and eyes set against a cool, clear complexion. This high contrast allows her to effortlessly pull off bold, intense colors like stark black, pure snow white, deep ruby red, and royal blue. Her elegant, classic "Dior" aesthetic is heavily rooted in this striking Winter Cool palette.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

Jisoo's visuals are legendary, but certain colors truly elevate her to goddess status.

*   **Best Styling (Winter Cool):** Jisoo looks her absolute best when there is high contrast. Think of her iconic stark black hair paired with a bold, cool-toned red lip. When she wears pure black, icy white, or vibrant jewel tones (like emerald or sapphire), her skin looks porcelain-clear, and her features become incredibly sharp and defined.
*   **Worst Styling (Autumn Warm):** During eras where she sported warm, orange-toned brown hair or wore heavy, earthy colors (like mustard or olive green), her skin lost its signature bright clarity, appearing slightly yellowish or fatigued. Winter Cools are easily overwhelmed by muted, warm tones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Why does Jisoo look so good in Dior?
Dior's classic aesthetic often revolves around high contrast—stark blacks, pure whites, and bold reds. This perfectly aligns with the Winter Cool palette, which thrives on deep, clear, and highly contrasting colors.

### 2. Do Winter Cools need heavy makeup?
Not necessarily. While they can handle bold lip colors better than anyone, a Winter Cool often looks stunning with very minimal eye makeup and just a striking, cool-toned lip to highlight their natural high contrast.
