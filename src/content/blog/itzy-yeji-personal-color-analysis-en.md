## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

To understand Yeji’s palette, you have to look at how different shades interact with her skin's natural undertones. As an Autumn Warm type, her skin possesses a rich, golden-beige baseline that thrives on warmth, depth, and muted saturation.

* **The Autumn Warm Magic (Best):** 
  * When Yeji steps out in rich espresso browns, soft warm caramel hair, and brick-red or terracotta lips, her complexion looks impossibly smooth and lit-from-within. 
  * Her signature cat-eye makeup executed in bronze, copper, and soft charcoal smoky shades enhances her eyes without looking harsh. 
  * Textured fabrics like suede, camel wool, and matte satin in golden undertones amplify her high-fashion aura.
* **The Cool-Tone Clash (Worst):** 
  * When styled in icy pastel blues, stark silver, or bubblegum pink—often seen in experimental concept shoots—her skin can instantly appear dull, tired, or slightly ashen. 
  * High-saturation neon brights overpower her natural elegance, shifting the focus away from her striking features and drawing attention straight to the clothing instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can an Autumn Warm type like Yeji ever wear black?**
While true black belongs in the Winter palette, Deep Autumns like Yeji can pull off darker tones if they lean toward charcoal or very deep espresso brown. If she wears black clothing, she balances it out instantly with warm-toned hair and rich terracotta or brick-red lip makeup to keep her complexion vibrant.

**How do I know if I share Yeji’s Autumn Warm personal color?**
If you look best in gold jewelry rather than silver, feel most radiant in earthy tones like olive, mustard, and rust, and find that warm peach or brick lipstick flatters your face more than hot pink, you likely share the same Autumn Warm classification as ITZY's superstar leader.
