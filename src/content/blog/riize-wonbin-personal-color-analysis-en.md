## TL;DR (Quick Answer)
RIIZE Wonbin (Park Wonbin) is the absolute epitome of **Summer Cool Mute/True**, possessing an ethereal, high-fashion translucency that demands a refined palette of cool ash-blacks, slate greys, and muted midnight navies. Warm, golden, or heavily saturated neon pigments instantly overwhelm his delicate dermal clarity, whereas cool-toned rose lips and smoky, desaturated textiles amplify his magnetic, 90s vintage guitar-hero aura.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

### The Flattering Palette (Summer Cool True/Mute)
When Wonbin is styled within his authentic Summer Cool domain, his complexion achieves a seamless, porcelain-like perfection. 
* **Red Carpet Excellence:** Picture him in a fluid, slate grey silk-blend shirt paired with a tailored midnight navy suit jacket. The cool undertones harmonize with his skin's natural pink-blue base, making his eyes appear sharper and his facial contours naturally chiseled without heavy contouring.
* **Music Video Magic:** Cool ash-black hair cascading in soft, effortless layers, accented by a subtle, cool-toned rose lip tint. This creates a striking yet understated contrast that feels expensive, artistic, and effortlessly chic.
* **Airport Fashion:** Oversized knitwear in foggy lavender-grey or desaturated denim jackets that emphasize his effortless, boyish cool without washing him out.

### The Draining Palette (Warm Autumn / Bright Spring)
When exposed to off-season warm or high-chroma palettes, Wonbin’s visual impact is instantly compromised.
* **The Warm Trap:** Mustard yellow, camel brown, or warm terracotta clothing casts an immediate sallow, yellowish shadow across his jawline, dulling the natural clarity of his skin.
* **The High-Chroma Overload:** Neon oranges or fiery warm reds fight for dominance against his features, making his skin look ruddy and shifting the focus away from his piercing gaze to the jarring fabric. 
* **Hair Missteps:** Golden blonde or warm copper hair dyes create a sharp visual dissonance with his cool skin undertones, making him look fatigued rather than ethereal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is RIIZE Wonbin (Park Wonbin) classified as a SUMMER COOL?
Wonbin possesses a delicate, high-contrast clarity where cool-toned, desaturated pigments elevate his entire presence. His skin reacts poorly to yellow and orange warmth, which introduces fatigue and sallowness. Conversely, the Summer Cool spectrum—characterized by foggy pastels, smoky greys, and muted jewel tones—allows his natural skin luminosity and sharp features to take center stage.

### What is their single most iconic red-carpet or music video styling?
His most iconic styling remains the effortless 90s rock-star look featuring tousled cool ash-black layers, minimal skin-blurring base makeup, a sheer cool-rose lip stain, and a structured midnight navy or slate grey layering piece. It perfectly encapsulates his signature "effortless cool" brand persona, balancing sharp rock-and-roll edge with timeless elegance.

### How can fans with a similar skin tone replicate this look daily?
To channel Wonbin's Summer Cool aesthetic, pivot your wardrobe away from warm earth tones and embrace a palette built around slate grey, charcoal, navy, and soft cool pinks. In your beauty routine, swap out coral and orange-based lip products for dusty roses and mauve tints, and opt for ash-toned brow and contour powders that keep your overall makeup look clean, modern, and perfectly cool-toned.
