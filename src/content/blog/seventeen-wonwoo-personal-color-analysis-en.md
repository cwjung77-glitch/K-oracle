## Frequently Asked Questions

### What exactly is a Winter Cool Personal Color type?

A Winter Cool individual has a cool undertone to their skin, hair, and eyes, and looks best in colors that are clear, vivid, and deep. They thrive in high-contrast palettes, often featuring true blacks, pure whites, icy pastels, and intense jewel tones like ruby, sapphire, and emerald. Their natural coloring often has a striking quality, and warm, muted, or earthy tones tend to diminish their vibrancy.

### Can Wonwoo ever wear warm colors?

While his best colors are cool, a Winter Cool person can sometimes incorporate *certain* warm colors if they are very clear and vivid, and used as an accent. For example, a deep, clear red (that leans slightly cool) might work better than a muted, earthy orange. However, generally, true warm tones will not be as harmonious and should be avoided for main garments or direct facial contact (e.g., lipstick, blush). The key is clarity and intensity over warmth and mutedness.

### How does Wonwoo's Personal Color affect his stage outfits and concepts?

Wonwoo's Winter Cool palette is incredibly advantageous for stage performance. The high-contrast colors and sharp lines associated with Winter Cool styling create a powerful, charismatic presence that commands attention. Concepts that lean into dark, sleek, or futuristic aesthetics with metallic silver accents or deep jewel tones would perfectly align with his Personal Color, making him look even more impactful under stage lights.

### What are some essential makeup tips for Winter Cool types like Wonwoo?

For Winter Cool types, the focus is on enhancing natural coolness and creating definition.
1.  **Cool-toned base:** Always choose foundations, concealers, and powders with a cool or neutral undertone.
2.  **Define with cool tones:** Use black or deep cool-toned eyeliners and mascaras for maximum impact. Eyeshadows in silver, grey, deep blues, or plums are excellent.
3.  **Berry or cool red lips:** These shades brighten the face and add a sophisticated pop of color.
4.  **Avoid warmth:** Steer clear of bronzers, warm gold highlighters, peachy blushes, and orange-toned lipsticks, as they can clash with the cool undertone.

### Does his skin tone change his Personal Color?

No, a person's Personal Color is determined by their *undertone*, which is constant regardless of how tan or pale their skin appears. While the depth of the recommended shades might slightly adjust with sun exposure (a tanned Winter Cool might pull off slightly deeper shades), the fundamental cool nature of their best colors remains the same. Wonwoo's cool undertone is inherent and unchanging.
