Welcome, K-Beauty enthusiasts and ONCEs, to another insightful Personal Color analysis from K-Oracle! Today, we're putting the spotlight on TWICE's beloved "Tofu" queen, Dahyun. Known for her bright smile, playful energy, and uniquely clear complexion, Dahyun effortlessly captures hearts. But what truly makes her glow?

At K-Oracle, our expert analysis confirms that Dahyun is a quintessential **Spring Warm (봄 웜톤)**. This vibrant and youthful palette perfectly complements her natural charm, enhancing her already luminous presence. Let's uncover the secrets behind her radiant styling!

## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

TWICE's Dahyun thrives in the **Spring Warm** palette. Her best colors are light, bright, and warm-toned, including peach, coral, ivory, light yellow, and mint green. She shines with fresh, natural makeup featuring peachy or warm pink lips and cheeks, and soft, golden-brown eye makeup. Warm-toned hair colors like honey blonde or light brown further illuminate her complexion, making her look youthful and incredibly vibrant.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

Observing Dahyun's various styling choices over the years provides compelling evidence of her Spring Warm classification.

**Best Styling: The Epitome of Spring Warm Radiance**

When Dahyun embraces her Spring Warm palette, her skin looks incredibly clear, luminous, and healthy. Her eyes sparkle, and her overall aura is one of youthful vitality.

*   **Colors:** Notice how she glows in **peach, coral, apricot, cream, ivory, light yellow, and soft mint green**. These colors pick up the natural warmth in her skin, making her complexion appear smoother and more translucent. A light coral dress, for instance, makes her look like she's bathed in sunlight, enhancing her natural flush rather than overwhelming it.
*   **Makeup:** Soft, fresh, and warm-toned makeup is her absolute best. **Peachy blush** placed high on the cheekbones gives her a youthful, healthy glow. **Warm brown or golden eyeshadows** define her eyes without harshness, making them appear brighter. **Coral or warm pink lip tints** add a natural pop of color that perfectly harmonizes with her skin tone, making her lips look plump and inviting.
*   **Hair:** Light to medium **warm brown, honey blonde, or even a soft orange/apricot tone** makes her complexion appear exceptionally bright and soft. These colors add a gentle warmth that frames her face beautifully, enhancing her "tofu"-like clarity.

**Worst Styling: When the Palette Clashes**

Conversely, when Dahyun strays into colors or tones that don't align with her Spring Warm nature, the effect can be less flattering, sometimes even draining.

*   **Colors:** True **black, stark white, cool navy, deep burgundy, or icy pinks** can make her skin appear dull, slightly sallow, or even cast subtle shadows. A harsh black outfit, for example, can dominate her delicate features and make her complexion look less vibrant, rather than providing a chic contrast. Her natural warmth struggles to shine through these cool or overly intense hues.
*   **Makeup:** Heavy, cool-toned makeup can be detrimental. **Smoky grey eyeshadows, cool-toned fuchsia lips, or overly strong contouring** can make her look tired, older, or even sickly. The contrast is too stark, and it diminishes her natural, fresh beauty. Her skin can appear uneven or overly made-up rather than naturally radiant.
*   **Hair:** Very dark, cool-toned hair colors like **jet black or ash brown/blonde** can wash out her complexion and make her features appear less defined. Instead of brightening her face, these colors can create a harsh contrast that detracts from her inherent warmth and clarity.
