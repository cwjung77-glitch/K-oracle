## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

If you have ever wondered how ILLIT's visual center Minju always manages to look like a glowing porcelain doll, the secret lies in her personal color profile. As a textbook **Light Spring (or bright Spring-leaning)**, Minju thrives in warm, vibrant, and clear pastel hues. 

*   **Her Season:** Light Spring (Warm & Bright undertones)
*   **Best Colors:** Peach, warm coral, strawberry milk pink, ivory, and bright butter yellow.
*   **Worst Colors:** Heavy gothic blacks, dusty grey-browns, and icy blue-based neon tones that wash out her natural warmth.
*   **Signature Vibe:** Fresh, dewy, youthful, and lively.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

To truly understand personal color, you have to look at how different shades interact with the skin. For Minju, the difference between her best and worst styling is night and day.

*   **The Best Styling (Light Spring Magic):** 
    When Minju wears airy peach tones, warm coral blush, and soft blonde-brown or warm chestnut hair, her skin instantly lights up. Her complexion looks clearer, her dark eyes pop with natural warmth, and she radiates a youthful, sparkling energy. She looks effortlessly expensive in clear, translucent pastel shades that have a hint of yellow warmth rather than icy blue undertones.
*   **The Worst Styling (Winter/Summer Mismatch):** 
    Have you ever seen her in heavy, jet-black clothing paired with stark white or cool-toned mauve lipstick? When forced into Winter or deep Autumn palettes, Minju’s face instantly takes on a grayish, tired shadow. Dark, heavy contours make her look older, and cool grey tones completely drain the lively peach undertones from her cheeks, making her look washed out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can Minju wear black clothes since she's an idol?**
K-pop styling often demands dark conceptual looks, and yes, Minju can still wear black! However, to prevent it from washing her out, she typically styles it with sheer fabrics, exposes her collarbones, or wears a warm-toned statement necklace to keep the dark color away from her face.

**How do I know if I share Minju's Light Spring personal color?**
If you look best in gold jewelry rather than silver, if bright peach lipstick flatters you more than blue-based Barbie pink, and if stark white makes you look pale while ivory warms you up, you might just share a palette with ILLIT's Minju!

**What is the biggest mistake Light Springs make?**
The most common mistake is wearing colors that are too heavy or dusty. Light Springs need clarity and lightness. Avoid muddy brown contour palettes and chalky purple-toned cool lipsticks.
