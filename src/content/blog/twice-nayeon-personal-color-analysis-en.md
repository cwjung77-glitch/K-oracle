## Frequently Asked Questions

* **Can Nayeon wear cool-toned makeup?**
  While she can pull off a neutral pink lip if the brightness level is high enough, truly cool, icy blue-based shades will detract from her natural warmth. If she wants to wear pink, it must be a warm, lively spring pink rather than a lavender or mauve.

* **What is the difference between Light Spring and Bright Spring?**
  Light Spring favors soft, pastel warm shades, while Bright Spring thrives on high-saturation, vivid warm colors. Nayeon sits comfortably on the border, meaning she can rock soft peach pastels just as well as punchy, high-energy coral-reds!

* **How can I test if I have the same personal color as Nayeon?**
  Hold a piece of bright orange fabric and a piece of hot pink fabric near your bare face under natural daylight. If your skin looks clearer, brighter, and more energized, you likely share a warm spring profile!
