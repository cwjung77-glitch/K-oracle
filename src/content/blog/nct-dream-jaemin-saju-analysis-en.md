## Deep Dive: The Core Energy

At the heart of Jaemin's Saju chart lies his **Day Master**, Gye (癸) Water, rooted in the Yu (酉) Rooster branch. Gye Water is often likened to gentle rain, dew, or a serene stream – adaptable, intuitive, and deeply empathetic. Individuals with Gye Water as their Day Master are known for their profound inner world, keen observation skills, and ability to connect with others on an emotional level. They possess a quiet strength, capable of eroding mountains over time, reflecting a persistent and resilient spirit beneath a calm exterior.

The Yu (酉) Rooster Earthly Branch, a pure Metal element, strongly supports his Gye Water. This combination, Gye Yu (계유), signifies water flowing from a precious metal spring or refined jewelry. It bestows exceptional intelligence, a sharp mind, and a natural affinity for arts and aesthetics. This strong Metal energy, further amplified by Gyeong (庚) and Sin (辛) Metal in his Year and Month Pillars, acts as a powerful "Resource Star" (印星, Inseong) for his Day Master. This suggests a person who is highly intelligent, a lifelong learner, and someone who receives significant support and guidance throughout his life. It also points to a strong sense of self-preservation and a refined taste.

However, the abundance of Metal also means a strong introspective tendency. While it fuels his artistic expression and analytical abilities, it can also lead to overthinking or a need for quiet solitude to recharge. The presence of Earth in his Year (Jin) and Month (Mi) Pillars, which control Water, represents "Officer/Power Stars" (官星, Gwanseong). This indicates a strong sense of responsibility, discipline, and a drive for achievement, but also potential pressure from external expectations or career demands.

Notably, Wood (Expression) and Fire (Wealth) elements are not overtly present in his main four pillars but are subtly hidden within the Earthly Branches (Jin contains Eul Wood, Mi contains Eul Wood and Jeong Fire). This implies that while his creativity and desire for personal wealth might be more internal or manifest through indirect channels, they are nevertheless vital components of his overall destiny.
