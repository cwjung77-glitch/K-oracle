## The Core Archetype: Refined Fire (Jeong Fire)

In Saju, the Day Master represents the true self. Yeji was born on a **Jeong-Chuk (Yin Fire Ox)** day. 

Unlike Yang Fire, which burns brightly and broadly like the midday sun, **Jeong Fire (丁火)** is the element of refined, concentrated heat. Think of a spotlight cutting through dark stage fog, a steady oil lamp, or a craftsman's precise torch. 

```
  Yin Fire (Jeong) = Precision, Warmth, Intense Stage Focus
         +
  Yin Earth (Chuk) = Grounded Discipline, Creativity, Endurance
```

This element gives Yeji remarkable qualities:

* **Laser-Focused Charisma:** She doesn't just radiate energy randomly; she channels every ounce of her soul directly into her performance.
* **Warmth Behind the Sharpness:** While her stage gaze can feel intimidatingly sharp, Jeong Fire possesses deep empathy, protecting and caring for those around her.
* **Unshakeable Artistic Standards:** Refined fire demands perfection. Yeji holds herself to extraordinarily high standards, practicing until every angle of her movement is flawless.
