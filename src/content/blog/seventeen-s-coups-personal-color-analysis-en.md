## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes S.Coups a classic Spring Warm type?
S.Coups exhibits the key characteristics of a Spring Warm: a naturally clear and bright complexion, often with golden or peachy undertones, and eyes that sparkle with warmth. He thrives in clear, vibrant, and light-to-medium warm colors that make him look energetic, approachable, and youthful. His natural warmth is amplified, not overshadowed, by these tones.

### Can Spring Warm types like S.Coups wear black?
While true black can be too harsh for a pure Spring Warm, it can be worn strategically. S.Coups can incorporate black as an accent (e.g., a slim belt, shoes, or a patterned detail) rather than a dominant color. If wearing a black garment, pairing it with warm accessories like gold jewelry, a warm-toned scarf, or a vibrant coral lip color can soften the effect and bring warmth closer to his face. Charcoal grey or a very dark warm brown are often better alternatives for full outfits.
