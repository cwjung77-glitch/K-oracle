## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between Spring Light and Spring Clear?**
Spring Light features delicate, pastel-heavy warmth, while Spring Clear (Bright Spring) leans slightly more saturated and vibrant. Rei sits comfortably in the Spring spectrum, shining brightest when she blends soft pastel warmth with clear, cheerful pops of color like coral and peach.

**Can I wear black if I am a Spring Warm like Rei?**
Stark black sits on the opposite spectrum of a warm, bright palette and can weigh down your features. If you love wearing dark clothes, try swapping pitch black for warm dark chocolate brown, deep navy, or camel, and always keep your makeup bright and peachy near the face.

**How can I tell if I share Rei's personal color?**
If you look best in gold jewelry rather than silver, feel energized by warm pastel colors like peach and mint green, and find that coral lipstick makes your face pop while pink lipstick makes you look gray, you likely share Rei's Spring Warm personal color classification.
