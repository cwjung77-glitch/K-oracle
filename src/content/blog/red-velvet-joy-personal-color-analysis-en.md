* **The Best Styling (Spring Bright Magic):** Joy shines brightest when draped in saturated, clear, and warm tones. ## Frequently Asked Questions

While jet black can sometimes feel a bit heavy for true Spring types, Joy can pull off sleek black outfits if her makeup relies on a vibrant coral lip and glowing skin to balance the darkness. However, warm navy, bright white, and camel will always look infinitely more harmonious on her.
* **What is the difference between Spring Warm and Autumn Warm?** 
Both are warm-toned, but Spring Warm (like Joy) requires *brightness* and high chroma. Autumn tones are muted, deep, and earthy. If Joy wears heavy autumn shades, her vibrant energy gets swallowed up by the clothes.

If you look best in gold jewelry, glow instantly when wearing fruit-punch red or bright peach, and find that pastel baby pinks or heavy grey-browns make you look washed out, you might just share Joy's gorgeous Spring Warm profile!
