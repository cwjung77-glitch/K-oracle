## Deep Dive: The Core Energy

At the heart of WayV Kun's Saju chart lies his **Day Master, Gyeong Jin (庚辰)**. This signifies a **Yang Metal (庚) person born on a Dragon (辰) day**. Imagine a vast, unyielding metal ore, a mighty sword in the making, or a grand, imposing rock formation. This Gyeong Metal embodies strength, determination, and an inherent resilience. It is a force that seeks refinement and transformation, always striving for its highest potential.

Sitting on the Jin (辰) Earthly Branch, which is a powerful Earth element, his Day Master is well-rooted and supported. This **Jin (Dragon) branch** also carries the energy of a **Hwa Gae Sal (華蓋殺)** for Gyeong Metal, a star associated with artistic talent, spirituality, and a deep, sometimes solitary, inner world. This combination suggests a person of profound introspection, creativity, and a unique perspective on life.

Let's break down the **Ten Gods (십신 - Sibsin)** to reveal more layers of his personality:

*   **Abundant Resources (印星 - In Seong) - Pyeon In (偏印)**: With Mu (戊) in his Month Stem and Jin (辰) in his Day Branch, Kun has strong Pyeon In energy. This indicates a highly intuitive, intelligent, and uniquely skilled individual. He thrives on unconventional knowledge, possesses a spiritual depth, and learns best through diverse experiences. This is the source of his profound problem-solving abilities and innovative thinking.
*   **Strong Expression (食傷 - Sik Sang) - Sik Sin (食神) & Sang Gwan (傷官)**: His chart boasts both Sik Sin (食神) from Hae (亥) and Sang Gwan (傷官) from Ja (子). This dual presence of expressive stars is a hallmark of a truly gifted artist and communicator. Sik Sin brings warmth, benevolence, and a natural flow of creativity, often associated with good fortune and a caring nature. Sang Gwan, on the other hand, grants him sharp intellect, innovation, and a desire to challenge norms. It's the spark of genius that pushes boundaries and allows him to stand out.
*   **Stable Wealth (財星 - Jae Seong) - Jeong Jae (正財)**: The Eul (乙) in his Year Stem represents Jeong Jae. This signifies diligence, practicality, and a responsible approach to finances and relationships. Kun is likely very systematic and hardworking when it comes to managing his resources and commitments, embodying a reliable and trustworthy character.

The prominent **Earth and Water elements** in his chart further amplify his core energies. Earth provides stability, grounding, and resourcefulness, while Water fuels his creativity, emotional depth, and adaptability. The interplay of these elements makes him a deeply thoughtful and expressive individual, capable of both immense focus and artistic fluidity.
