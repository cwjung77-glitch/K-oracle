## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

IVE's center, Jang Wonyoung, is the ultimate representation of a **True Summer Cool (여름 쿨톤 트루)**. Her skin has a soft, cool undertone that radiates when paired with pastel pinks, lavender, and icy blues. By avoiding heavy, warm colors like mustard yellow or deep orange, she maintains her signature clear, "strawberry milk" aesthetic that has taken K-Pop by storm.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

When we analyze Wonyoung's styling throughout her career, the impact of her personal color becomes undeniable.

*   **Best Styling (Summer Cool):** Think of her "Love Dive" and "Eleven" eras. When styled in light, grayish pastels, silver jewelry, and cool-toned pink lips, her complexion looks instantly brighter and flawless. The coolness of the colors harmonizes with her skin's natural undertone, making her features pop.
*   **Worst Styling (Autumn Warm):** Occasionally, she has been styled in deep, warm autumn colors—like earthy browns or warm brick reds. While she is always beautiful, these heavy, warm shades tend to cast a slight yellow or dull shadow over her naturally bright skin, causing her to lose that ethereal, glassy glow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Can a Summer Cool wear black?
Yes, but it shouldn't be too heavy. While True Winters look striking in deep, solid black, Summer Cools look better when black is balanced with lighter, airy fabrics or worn away from the face. Wonyoung often wears black with open necklines to keep her complexion bright.

### 2. What is the difference between Summer Light and Summer True?
Summer Light favors the palest, most pastel colors, while Summer True (like Wonyoung) can handle slightly more saturation, like a classic cool fuchsia or a soft, medium berry, as long as it strictly lacks any yellow warmth.
