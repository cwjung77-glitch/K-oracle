## TL;DR (Quick Answer)
ITZY’s powerhouse dancer Chaeryeong is the ultimate muse for **Autumn Warm Mute (Soft Autumn)**. Her skin possesses a delicate, creamy undertone with a muted golden warmth that instantly wilts under high-contrast or icy winter palettes. When styled in sophisticated cinnamon milk tea tones, cashmere textures, and antique rose-brick gradients, her natural grace is elevated into high-fashion editorial perfection.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

### The Flattering Palette: Autumn Warm Mute Excellence
Chaeryeong achieves her most ethereal, expensive-looking aesthetic when draped in desaturated, warm-leaning neutrals. 
* **Red Carpet:** Think fluid silk gowns in antique bronze, roasted almond, and warm taupe that melt seamlessly into her complexion without overpowering her features.
* **Airport Fashion:** Oversized hazelnut blazers layered over cream ribbed knitwear, paired with muted olive or moss green accessories. 
* **Music Videos:** In cinematic concepts, she radiates when styled in warm caramel hair extensions, sun-kissed terracotta contours, and dusty peach-brown eyeshadows that emphasize her elegant eye shape.

### The Draining Palette: High-Contrast & Icy Clashes
When placed in Winter Cool or Bright Spring palettes, Chaeryeong's natural harmony is instantly disrupted.
* **The Pitfalls:** Neon brights, stark snow whites, jet black, and piercing fuchsia pinks create an unnatural shadow beneath her jawline, making her creamy skin look sallow and tired.
* **The Misstep:** Icy silver highlights or blue-based magenta lip tints completely wash out her warmth, shifting focus away from her refined facial structure and onto the harsh clothing pigment instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is ITZY Chaeryeong classified as an AUTUMN WARM?
Chaeryeong’s skin features a subtle, low-saturation golden undertone that harmonizes best with earthy, muted pigments rather than saturated primaries or cool pastels. Her features possess a soft, smoky depth that looks effortlessly chic when paired with autumn’s signature warm, golden-brown spectrum. 

### What is their single most iconic red-carpet or music video styling?
Her most unforgettable visual era centers around her warm caramel-brown hair styling paired with a monochromatic beige-and-cinnamon wardrobe. During this period, her makeup featured a signature soft-brick lip gradient and hazy terracotta eyeshadow that defined the pinnacle of Cheongdam-dong soft elegance.

### How can fans with a similar skin tone replicate this look daily?
To channel Chaeryeong's Autumn Warm Mute aesthetic, swap out harsh black eyeliners for deep espresso brown, and replace blue-based pink lipsticks with muted roasted-almond or soft terracotta shades. Focus on layering matte and satin textures in warm neutral tones like oatmeal, camel, and olive for an instant upscale transformation.
