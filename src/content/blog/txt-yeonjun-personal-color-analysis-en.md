## TL;DR (Quick Answer)
As the undisputed 4th-generation IT boy, TXT’s Yeonjun commands the global fashion stage through an innate, high-end magnetism anchored by an official **Autumn Warm (True/Deep)** personal color profile. His rich skin undertones, sharp jawline, and commanding stage presence thrive not in icy silvers or stark pastels, but in the decadent, earth-infused palette of the golden season. From toasted caramel hair to rich brick-red lips and enveloping spiced camel trench coats, Yeonjun’s aesthetic is the ultimate masterclass in modern luxury styling.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

### The Autumn Warm Triumph (Best)
Yeonjun’s visual peak is defined by depth, warmth, and textural richness. When he steps out in a tailored, oversized spiced camel trench coat layered over a warm terracotta cashmere knit, his skin radiates a healthy, expensive glow. Rich mahogany, burnt orange, espresso brown, and muted olive amplify his natural contrast, sharpening his features and giving his complexion a seamless, lit-from-within warmth. 

### The Muted Misstep (Worst)
Conversely, icy platinums, stark optical whites, and pastel lavenders drain the vibrant life right out of his complexion. When styled in cool-toned silver accessories or chalky blue separates, Yeonjun’s skin instantly appears sallow, and the natural golden warmth of his facial structure turns flat and fatigued. High-contrast neon or frosted hues overpower his sophisticated features, clashing violently with his rich undertones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is TXT Yeonjun (Choi Yeonjun) classified as an AUTUMN WARM?
Yeonjun’s skin possesses a rich, golden-olive undertone that effortlessly absorbs and reflects warm light. His features hold a natural depth and high-to-medium contrast, allowing him to carry heavy, saturated earthy pigments—such as rust, burnt sienna, and deep bronze—without looking weighed down. Autumn Warm tones harmonize completely with his natural pigment architecture, creating a unified, high-fashion aesthetic.

### What is their single most iconic red-carpet or music video styling?
His legendary airport and music video appearances featuring the monochrome spiced camel trench coat paired with textured, toasted caramel locks remain unmatched. This look perfectly bridges effortless model-off-duty styling with meticulous runway tailoring, cementing his status as a global style icon.

### How can fans with a similar skin tone replicate this look daily?
Swap out stark black and cool-toned grey basics for rich espresso browns, warm olives, and mustard yellows. For daily makeup, ditch cool-toned pink glosses in favor of a terracotta-tinted lip balm and a light dusting of bronze contour across the cheekbones to instantly elevate your natural warmth.
