## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between Autumn Mute and Deep Autumn for Giselle?**
Giselle occupies a versatile space within the Autumn Warm spectrum. While her deep, soulful eyes allow her to pull off dramatic Deep Autumn styling (like rich espresso hair and dark chocolate coats), her overall complexion thrives best with the sophisticated desaturation of Autumn Mute (think dusty rose, muted mocha, and soft terracotta). 

**Can Autumn Warm idols wear black?**
While pure, pitch-black can sometimes feel a bit heavy against an Autumn Warm complexion, Giselle often bypasses this by opting for charcoal, dark espresso, or styled black outfits paired with warm-toned makeup, gold jewelry, and rich hair color to balance the contrast.

**How can I tell if I share Giselle's Autumn Warm personal color?**
