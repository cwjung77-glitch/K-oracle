## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can an Autumn Warm Mute wear black?**
While true black is technically a cool, high-contrast winter color, Kazuha occasionally wears it by keeping her makeup extra warm and using sheer, lightweight fabrics. However, swapping stark black for deep charcoal gray or dark espresso brown instantly softens her features and looks significantly more harmonious.

**How do I know if I am an Autumn Warm Mute like Kazuha?**
If silver jewelry makes your skin look a bit gray or tired, but gold gives you a healthy radiance, you likely have warm undertones. If you look fantastic in muted, dusty, or earthy colors rather than neon brights or pastel ice creams, you belong right alongside Kazuha in the Autumn Warm family.

**What is the biggest mistake to avoid with this personal color?**
The biggest trap is choosing colors that are either too frosty or too intensely saturated. Avoid anything neon, icy, or starkly primary. Always lean toward shades that look like they have a drop of warm gray, beige, or brown mixed into them.
