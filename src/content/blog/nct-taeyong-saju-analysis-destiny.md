To understand Lee Taeyong’s magnetic stage presence and untamed artistic spirit, one must look at his Day Master—the foundational energy of a Saju chart. Born on July 1, 1995, Taeyong’s core identity is **Yang Fire (丙, Byeong)** sitting atop the **Horse (午, Oh)**. 

In ancient energetic reading, Yang Fire represents the sun itself. It illuminates everything in its path, radiates heat without asking for anything in return, and commands attention effortlessly. When paired with the Horse—a peak Fire branch—it creates a rare dynamic known as the **Red Fire Horse**.

* **Blazing Charisma:** He doesn't just enter a room; he brightens it. The sun cannot be hidden, which explains why his visual brilliance and stage aura were evident from his pre-debut days.
* **Uncompromising Passion:** The double Fire energy gives him endless stamina for perfectionism in dance, rap, fashion, and songwriting.
* **The Yang Blade Effect:** Sitting on the Horse branch creates a spiritual dynamic called *Yang-In* (the Yang Blade). This grants him an unbreakable will, extreme resilience under pressure, and a fiery pride that drives him to master any skill he touches.

## The Clash That Created a Leader: Fire vs. Water

A chart composed purely of Fire would burn out quickly or become overly reckless. What makes Taeyong’s Saju remarkable is the dramatic tension between his dominant **Fire** energy and his influential **Water** elements.

In his Month Pillar sits **Yang Water (壬, Im)**. Water represents the *7 Killings* or *Direct Officer* star in traditional reading—the force of discipline, self-restraint, rules, and immense public responsibility.

* **Leadership Through Sacrifice:** Yang Water sits directly over the Horse's Fire. Fire wants to express freely, but Water demands structure and accountability. This friction makes him an deeply conscientious leader who puts the collective safety and success of NCT ahead of his own comfort.
* **Emotional Depth in Artistry:** The collision of Fire and Water creates steam—an intangible, mist-like atmosphere. This elemental reaction translates directly into his surreal, avant-garde concepts, haunting lyrics, and multi-layered choreography choices.
