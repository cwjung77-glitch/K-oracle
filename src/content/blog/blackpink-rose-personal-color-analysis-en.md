## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can a Light Spring pull off dark hair?**
While Rosé is world-famous for her iconic blonde locks, she has occasionally rocked a soft, warm medium-brown. However, jet-black hair often creates too high of a contrast against her delicate skin, making her look stark rather than harmonious. If she goes dark, it needs to be a soft, warm cocoa shade.

**What is the difference between Light Spring and Bright Spring?**
Light Spring palettes are dominated by lightness and softness—think pastels with a warm undertone. Bright Spring palettes require higher contrast and more saturated, vivid colors. Rosé leans primarily into Light Spring because she shines brightest in delicate, airy shades rather than neon brights.

**How can I test if I have the same personal color as Rosé?**
Hold a piece of warm peach fabric and a piece of cool fuchsia fabric under your natural-light selfie. If the peach fabric makes your skin look clearer and minimizes dark circles while the fuchsia makes you look ashy, you likely share a warm Spring or Autumn palette!
