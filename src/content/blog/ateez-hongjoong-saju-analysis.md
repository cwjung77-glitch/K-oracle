## The Core Element: Jeong Fire (The Torch in the Night)

In the Four Pillars of Destiny, every person has a Day Master—the central star of their chart. For Kim Hongjoong, that core is **Jeong Fire**. 

Unlike the overwhelming heat of the blazing sun (Byeong Fire), Jeong Fire represents flickering candlelight, a campfire beneath the stars, or a beacon guiding ships through stormy seas. It is gentle on the surface, yet remarkably enduring. 

* **Quiet Fire:** Jeong Fire individuals rarely scream for attention. Instead, their presence draws people in organically. They illuminate the path for others.
* **Meticulous Focus:** This element grants an extraordinary attention to detail. Whether adjusting a song's mixing by a single decibel or customizing a stage outfit, Hongjoong's inner flame burns with laser focus.
* **Resilient Passion:** Candlelight doesn't burn out easily when protected. It adapts to the room, bending with the wind without disappearing.

This explains why Hongjoong's energy on stage is intensely captivating. He does not force his authority; he radiates a concentrated magnetic force that demands respect.
