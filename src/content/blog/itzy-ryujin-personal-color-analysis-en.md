## TL;DR (Quick Answer)
ITZY’s powerhouse center Ryujin is officially classified as a **Spring Warm** (specifically bridging Spring Bright and Spring Light). While many idols in her lane are pushed into stark, high-contrast winters, Ryujin’s natural skin clarity, golden undertones, and lively vitality thrive in warm, luminous, and clear color palettes. Her iconic aesthetic relies on glowing peach-coral lips, translucent skin finishes, and warm dimensional hair that enhances her signature effortless girl-crush charisma without washing out her natural warmth.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

### The Spring Warm Sweet Spot (Best Styling)
Ryujin reaches her absolute visual peak when draped in clear, high-chroma warm tones that mirror her dynamic energy. 
* **Music Video Styling:** In high-energy comebacks, warm honey blonde or rich chestnut brown hair instantly illuminates her complexion. Pairing this with a vibrant, semi-matte coral-peach lip and a dusting of sun-kissed apricot blush creates a healthy, lit-from-within glow.
* **Airport & Casual Chic:** Creamy ivories, warm camel coats, and butter-yellow knits soften her features, lending an approachable yet luxurious model-off-duty texture. 
* **Red Carpet Elegance:** Metallic accents in champagne gold or warm rose-gold sequins amplify her natural skin radiance, ensuring she commands the flashbulbs with a youthful, fresh-faced vitality.

### The Pitfalls (Worst Styling)
Because Ryujin possesses a delicate, clear warmth, heavy or icy colors instantly disrupt her natural harmony.
* **The Winter Trap:** Stark, icy platinum hair or stark black clothing drains the golden undertones from her skin, casting a grayish shadow over her jawline and making her look fatigued.
* **Heavy Muted Tones:** Dusty rose, mauve, or heavy, muddy taupes rob her face of its signature youthful bounce, making her vibrant features look flat and lackluster.
* **Cool Blue-Bases:** Blue-based fuchsias or stark lavender lips clash violently with her natural warmth, instantly aging her aesthetic and creating an unnatural, chalky finish.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is ITZY Ryujin classified as a SPRING WARM?
Ryujin possesses clear, golden-undertoned skin that reacts brilliantly to light, warm, and moderately bright colors. Unlike Autumn types who require deep, muted warmth, or Winters who need high-contrast icy tones, Ryujin's natural vibrancy and clear complexion require crisp, fresh warm hues to look alive and energized. Her skin clarity and youthful radiance are the definitive markers of a Spring Warm profile.

### What is their single most iconic red-carpet or music video styling?
Ryujin’s most legendary visual era features her rocking a dimensional honey-blonde bob paired with a glowing, translucent peach makeup look. This styling perfectly captures her signature duality—effortlessly cool yet vibrantly warm—making it an instant benchmark for K-Beauty styling in the Cheongdam-dong aesthetic circle.

### How can fans with a similar skin tone replicate this look daily?
To channel Ryujin's Spring Warm glow in your everyday routine, ditch heavy matte foundations in favor of satin, glowing bases. Swap out cool blue-red lipsticks for juicy peach and bright coral tints. For hair, steer away from ash or black dyes and opt for warm caramel or honey-brown dimensions that catch the light and instantly brighten your face.
