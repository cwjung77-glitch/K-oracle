## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can Sana wear cool-toned pink lipstick?**
Because Sana sits comfortably in the Light Spring category, she can pull off neutral-leaning pinks, but blue-based magenta or hot fuchsia lipsticks will look disconnected from her face. If she wears pink, it *must* have a warm, milky undertone—think strawberry milk rather than bubblegum blue-pink.

**How do I know if I have the same personal color as Sana?**
If you look best in gold jewelry over silver, if white fabric makes your skin glow compared to an off-cream or ivory, and if pastel peach blushes look more natural on you than mauve or rose, you might just share Sana's Light Spring color palette!

**Does Sana ever suit dark hair?**
While her absolute best looks feature warm, dimensional blonde or rich apricot-brown hair, she can rock a soft, espresso-toned dark brown as long as it has a warm, glossy finish. However, jet-black hair tends to overpower her delicate facial features.
