## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

Red Velvet's Irene is widely considered one of K-Pop's ultimate visual centers, and she is a classic example of a **True Summer Cool (여름 쿨톤 트루)**. Her beauty is defined by a clear, transparent, and slightly pinkish skin tone that looks incredibly elegant and pure when paired with soft, cool colors. Pastel pinks, airy lavenders, and cool grayish-blues bring out a natural, ethereal glow that is her signature look.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

Irene's long career provides perfect examples of Summer Cool styling.

*   **Best Styling (Summer Cool):** Irene's most breathtaking looks involve light, cool, and airy colors. When she wears soft lavender, powder blue, or a cool-toned rosy pink lip, her skin looks like porcelain. The lack of heavy contrast or warm yellowness allows her delicate features to shine brilliantly.
*   **Worst Styling (Autumn/Spring Warm):** During certain concepts where she wore heavy orange blush, warm coral lips, or deep earthy clothing, her natural clarity was overshadowed. Warm, heavy colors can make a Summer Cool's skin look ruddy or flushed in an unflattering way, hiding their natural transparency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Why is Irene considered the standard for Summer Cool?
Because her skin has a very distinct coolness and clarity that reacts dramatically to color. When she wears her correct palette, she literally seems to glow, proving the massive impact of finding the right personal color.

### 2. Can Summer Cools wear dark red lipstick?
Summer Cools generally look better in softer, blended colors rather than stark, heavy colors. Instead of a deep, opaque matte red (which suits Winter Cools), Irene looks better in a sheer, glossy cool berry or a soft, smudged rosy red.
