## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

BLACKPINK's Jennie, famously known as "Human Chanel," radiates an effortlessly luxurious vibe that stems from her **Autumn Warm (가을 웜톤)** personal color. Specifically leaning towards Autumn Mute or Deep, Jennie shines in rich, warm, and sophisticated shades like camel, khaki, warm beige, and brick red. Her natural golden undertones allow her to carry heavy, luxurious textures and warm, muted colors with unmatched chicness.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

Jennie is a trendsetter, and her most viral looks almost always align with her warm palette.

*   **Best Styling (Autumn Warm):** Jennie's best looks involve warm, earthy tones and low contrast. Her signature nude or soft brick lip colors, paired with warm brown or subtly highlighted hair, make her look incredibly sophisticated. When she wears beige, warm tweed (very Chanel), or rich gold jewelry, she glows with a healthy, luxurious aura.
*   **Worst Styling (Winter Cool):** While Jennie can pull off almost anything with her attitude, extremely icy, stark colors like neon blue or bright, cool fuchsia can sometimes look separate from her face, floating above her skin rather than blending harmoniously with her warm undertones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is Jennie a Spring Warm or Autumn Warm?
There is often debate, as she can pull off some bright Spring colors. However, her most iconic, harmonious, and sophisticated looks (her nude lips, tweed jackets, and smoky brown eyes) belong firmly in the Autumn Warm palette. She has the depth to handle muted, rich colors that would wash out a Spring Warm.

### 2. How does Jennie wear black so well if she is Autumn Warm?
Jennie often wears "warm black" or mixes black with warm textures (like tweed or knit). She also heavily utilizes warm-toned makeup and gold jewelry to inject warmth back into her look when wearing black.
