## Frequently Asked Questions

**What exactly is a "Deep Winter" personal color?**
Deep Winter (also known as Dark Winter) is a sub-season characterized by dark hair and eyes paired with cool or neutral-cool undertones. The defining trait of this palette is *depth* and *clarity*. People in this category handle heavy, dark, and rich colors effortlessly without looking overwhelmed.

**Can Ningning wear warm makeup tones?**
While strict personal color rules suggest avoiding warmth, top-tier celebrity stylists sometimes break the rules for a specific concept. However, whenever Ningning wears warm orange-corals or muddy browns, it requires heavy filtering or expert lighting to look right. In real life, cool tones naturally harmonize best with her features.

**How can I figure out if I share Ningning's personal color?**
Check your jewelry and your skin's reaction to fabric drapes! If silver jewelry makes your skin look clearer and more awake compared to gold, and if jewel tones make your eyes sparkle while earthy colors make you look shadowy, you might just be a fellow Winter type like aespa's powerhouse vocalist.
