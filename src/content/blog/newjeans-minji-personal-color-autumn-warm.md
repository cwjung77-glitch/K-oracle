## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

NewJeans' Minji possesses a classic, earthy elegance that perfectly aligns with an **Autumn Warm (가을 웜톤)** personal color. Unlike the bright, flashy colors typical of K-Pop, Minji's natural, understated beauty shines brightest in muted, warm tones like olive green, beige, warm brown, and soft brick red. Her styling often emphasizes this natural warmth, giving her a timeless, nostalgic vibe.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

Minji's styling is a masterclass in embracing one's natural coloring.

*   **Best Styling (Autumn Warm):** Minji looks absolutely stunning in natural, earthy tones. Her iconic look featuring straight, natural dark brown hair, paired with muted coral or soft brick lips, highlights her healthy, glowing skin. Colors like khaki, camel, and muted mustard make her look effortlessly chic and grounded.
*   **Worst Styling (Winter Cool):** If Minji were to wear harsh, icy colors like neon blue, stark fuchsia, or heavy silver, it would clash with her skin's natural golden undertone. It would make her complexion appear slightly gray or washed out, overpowering her natural, soft features.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is Minji an Autumn Mute or Autumn Deep?
Minji leans towards **Autumn Mute (가을 뮤트)**. She looks best in colors that are slightly softened and blended, rather than extremely dark or highly saturated colors. This is why her "no-makeup" makeup looks are so legendary.

### 2. Can Autumn Warms wear pastels?
Traditional icy pastels should be avoided, but Autumn Warms can wear "warm pastels" or muted light colors, such as oatmeal, warm ivory, or a very muted, dusty peach.
