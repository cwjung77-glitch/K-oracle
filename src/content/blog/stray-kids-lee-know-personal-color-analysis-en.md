## Frequently Asked Questions

### What exactly does Winter Cool mean for a person's overall look?
Winter Cool individuals have a cool skin undertone, often appearing porcelain-like, olive, or deep. Their natural hair is typically dark (black, dark brown), and their eyes are usually dark or clear and bright. They look best in colors that are clear, vibrant, and highly saturated, with a distinct cool temperature. High contrast is key to their aesthetic, making them shine in colors like true black, white, deep jewel tones, and icy pastels.

### Can Winter Cools ever wear warm colors?
While their best colors are cool, Winter Cools can sometimes incorporate *small accents* of warm colors if they are very clear and vibrant, like a true ruby red (which has a cool base), rather than a warm brick red. However, generally, true warm colors (like orange, olive green, golden yellow) tend to make them look sallow or dull. It's best to stick to their dominant cool palette for major pieces and makeup.

### Why is black such a good color for Winter Cools, when it can be harsh on others?
Black is one of the absolute best colors for Winter Cools because it perfectly matches their high-contrast nature. It provides a stark, clear backdrop that makes their skin look brighter, their eyes more defined, and their features sharper. Unlike other seasons that might be overwhelmed or dulled by black, Winter Cools are invigorated by its intensity and clarity.
