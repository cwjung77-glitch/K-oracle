## TL;DR (Quick Answer)
SEVENTEEN’s Joshua (Hong Jisoo) is the quintessential embodiment of **Summer Cool Mute**. Possessing a delicate, refined neutral-cool undertone with soft clarity, Joshua’s natural aura thrives away from heavy warmth or stark neon contrasts. By anchoring his wardrobe and beauty looks in misty, desaturated hues—such as dusty rose, pale sky-blue, and muted lavender—he achieves that signature ethereal, aristocratic "gentle prince" aesthetic that defines his global style icon status.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

### The Masterclass: Best Styling (Summer Cool Mute)
When Joshua steps out in tailored pale sky-blue linen suits or soft, dusty rose knitwear, his skin instantly clarifies, and his features harmonize with poetic precision. 
* **Tailoring & Textures:** Matte, breathable fabrics like raw silk, fine-gauge merino wool, and lightweight linen in overcast gray-blues or misty lilacs amplify his ethereal softness.
* **Hair & Jewelry:** Cool ash-brown hair paired with understated white gold or platinum accessories illuminates his complexion without overpowering his delicate facial structure.

### The Pitfall: Worst Styling (Warm Autumn / Winter Bright)
Conversely, when styled in heavy Autumn palettes—such as burnt orange, mustard yellow, or tomato red—Joshua's skin absorbs the warmth, resulting in a fatigued, yellowish cast. 
* **The Contrast Clash:** High-contrast Winter brights, like jet-black hair paired with stark optic white or neon fuchsia, completely fracture his face, drawing attention to the clothing rather than his natural, refined features. True harmony for Joshua requires desaturation and gentle, foggy undertones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is SEVENTEEN Joshua (Hong Jisoo) classified as a SUMMER COOL?
Joshua’s skin possesses a delicate cool-to-neutral undertone that reacts poorly to heavy yellow or golden pigments. His optimal palette belongs to the Summer Cool Mute category because his features are best framed by muted, powdery, and mist-infused tones rather than high-saturation brights or deep earthy weights. This coloring allows him to project a calm, approachable, yet impossibly sophisticated aura.

### What is their single most iconic red-carpet or music video styling?
His legendary styling during the *Left & Right* era, featuring a soft ash-brown fringe paired with an oversized, muted lavender knit cardigan, remains a masterclass in K-pop fashion. It visually translates the Summer Cool Mute aesthetic into a modern, approachable luxury standard that instantly went viral across global fashion communities.

### How can fans with a similar skin tone replicate this look daily?
To channel Joshua's signature Cheongdam-dong aesthetic, banish heavy warm bronzers and replace them with cool-toned taupe contours and mauve-tinted lip balms. Build your wardrobe around foundational neutrals like slate gray, misty blue, and soft rose, focusing on tactile, matte fabrics that reflect light softly rather than aggressively.
