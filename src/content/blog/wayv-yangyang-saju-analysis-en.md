## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Day Master in Saju?
Your Day Master (Ilgan, 日干) is the Heavenly Stem of your Day Pillar, representing your core self, personality, and fundamental energy. It is the most crucial element in a Saju reading, acting as the lens through which all other elements of your chart are interpreted. For Yangyang, his Day Master is Gye (癸) Water.

### How does Yangyang's Saju suggest his career path?
Yangyang's Saju, particularly his strong Earth (Officer/Power) and Fire (Wealth) elements, strongly indicates a career path involving public roles, leadership, and significant achievement. The abundance of Officer/Power energy points to a destiny where he will be recognized, hold positions of responsibility, and thrive in structured, high-pressure environments. His charisma (Wealth energy) makes him a natural performer, while his creative Metal (Resource) allows him to bring unique artistry to his work.

### What are the unique strengths of a Gye Water Day Master?
Gye Water Day Masters are known for their exceptional adaptability, deep intuition, and intelligence. They are often empathetic, perceptive, and possess a quiet strength that allows them to navigate complex situations. Their ability to "flow" makes them resilient and resourceful, and they often have a calming yet influential presence. They are also skilled communicators and possess a profound understanding of human emotions.

### How reliable is a Saju reading without knowing the exact birth hour?
While the Hour Pillar (Sigju, 時柱) provides significant detail, especially regarding one's later life, children, and internal thoughts, a Saju reading based on the Year, Month, and Day Pillars (which are known) is still highly reliable and insightful. These three pillars already account for 75% of the chart's energy, offering a comprehensive understanding of one's core personality, family background, social environment, and career trajectory. For public figures like Yangyang, the known pillars alone reveal a rich tapestry of destiny.
