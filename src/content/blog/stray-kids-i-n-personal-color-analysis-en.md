## Frequently Asked Questions

### What exactly is a Summer Cool Personal Color?
Summer Cool (여름 쿨톤) is one of the four main Personal Color seasons, characterized by cool undertones, typically light to medium contrast, and a preference for soft, muted, and somewhat pastel shades. Individuals with this coloring look best in colors that are calming, refreshing, and reminiscent of a hazy summer day, like sky blue, lavender, dusty rose, and cool gray.

### Can Summer Cool individuals like I.N. ever wear black?
Yes, Summer Cools can wear black, but it often looks best when styled carefully. Pure black can sometimes be too stark or heavy for their soft, muted essence. For I.N., black works well when paired with a lighter, cool-toned color near his face (e.g., a white shirt under a black jacket) or when the fabric has a softer texture. Charcoal gray or cool dark navy can often be more flattering alternatives, offering similar depth without the intensity of true black.

### What kind of makeup should a Summer Cool avoid?
Summer Cools should generally avoid makeup shades with strong warm or golden undertones. This includes warm orange or coral blushes, golden bronze eyeshadows, and warm brown or peachy lipsticks. These colors can clash with their cool skin, making them appear sallow, tired, or emphasizing redness. Instead, opting for cool-toned alternatives will ensure a more harmonious and flattering look.

While online guides offer a good starting point, the most accurate way to find your Personal Color is through a professional consultation with a certified analyst.
