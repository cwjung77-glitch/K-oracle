Pyeon-Gwan represents intense career challenges, leadership opportunities, high pressure, and a need for strong self-discipline. This Metal element, clashing with his Wood, can be demanding, but it also forges resilience and offers immense opportunities for significant career breakthroughs and recognition. It pushes him to overcome obstacles and emerge stronger, often leading to a powerful, authoritative presence.

The Earth element (Gi) acts as a grounding force, ensuring his efforts lead to tangible rewards.

While his current Daewoon brings Metal, the inherent lack of Water suggests he might rely more on practical experience and intuition rather than purely academic knowledge. The initial lack of Metal means he naturally thrives with creative freedom but learns to embrace discipline and structure through his career path, particularly through his Daewoon.
