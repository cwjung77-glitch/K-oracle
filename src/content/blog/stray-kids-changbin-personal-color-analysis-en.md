## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

Changbin is a **Spring Warm (봄 웜톤)** color type. This means he harmonizes best with bright, warm, and clear colors that emphasize his youthful glow and golden undertone. Think peach, coral, warm beige, and vibrant greens.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

### Best Stylings for a Spring Warm Type

Changbin shines brightest in colors that reflect his natural warmth.

*   **Clothing:** Light ivory or cream tones, warm beige and camel shades, delicate peach and coral colors, as well as vibrant yellows and apple greens make him look fresh and energetic. A bright, warm denim blue or a fresh mint green are also excellent. These colors make his complexion glow and emphasize the golden nuance of his skin. For example, when he wears a light, cream-colored shirt, his face immediately appears more awake and his eyes brighter.
*   **Hair:** Warm brown shades like honey brown, milk tea, or a light golden brown are ideal. They perfectly complement his skin tone without overpowering it or making it appear harsh. Even a subtle, warm reddish tint can look fantastic on him.
*   **Makeup:** A warm peach blush, golden or peach-toned eyeshadows, and lip colors in coral or warm pink underscore his youthful vitality. These shades blend seamlessly with his complexion, giving him a healthy, radiant look.

### Less Ideal Stylings for a Spring Warm Type

Conversely, certain colors can make Changbin look sallow or tired, as they clash with his warm undertone.

*   **Clothing:** Cool black, deep navy blue, cool gray, or very dark, muted colors can overwhelm his natural warmth. Strong, pure white can also appear too harsh and make him look pale, as it creates too strong a contrast with his warm complexion.
*   **Hair:** Very dark, cool black or platinum blonde with an ashy undertone can make him look harsh and unnatural. These colors strip away his softness and freshness.
*   **Makeup:** Cool pink, purple, or silver on the eyes or lips can make his skin appear sallow. Too strong a cool contrast can make his facial features appear disharmonious and diminish his radiance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a Spring Warm type wear black?

Yes, but with caution. Pure black can often appear too harsh and make a Spring Warm type look sallow. It's better to use black as an accent, for example in accessories or as part of a pattern. If black is worn, it should be combined with a warm color close to the face, like a peach-colored scarf or a warm top under a black blazer, to soften the contrast and maintain warmth.

### Is Changbin a Spring Warm type, even though he often wears dark clothing?

Absolutely! Personal Color analysis is based on the natural undertones of skin, hair, and eyes, which do not change. Artists like Changbin often experiment with various stylings for concepts or performances. Even when he wears dark clothing, you'll notice that his best looks often incorporate warm makeup tones or hair colors that complement his natural Spring Warm palette and enhance his radiance.
