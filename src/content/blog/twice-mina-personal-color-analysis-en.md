## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can Mina wear any warm colors at all?**
A: Generally, true Cool Winters look best staying away from yellow-based colors. However, if she wears warm tones, she balances them with heavy cool-toned makeup to prevent her face from looking washed out. For everyday wear, sticking to cool pastels or deep jewel tones is always the safest bet.

**Q: What is the difference between Cool Winter and Cool Summer?**
A: Cool Summers thrive in soft, muted, powdery pastel shades (think lavender and dusty rose). Cool Winters, on the other hand, shine in high-contrast, vivid, or icy-clear colors (like pure white, black, and sapphire blue). Mina leans heavily toward Winter because she rocks high-contrast styling effortlessly.

**Q: How do I know if I have the same personal color as Mina?**
A: Hold a piece of pure white paper and a piece of cream-colored paper up to your bare face in natural light. If the pure white paper makes your skin look clearer and your eyes pop, you likely lean cool. If silver jewelry makes your skin glow more than gold, you are a great candidate for a cool-toned palette like Mina's!
