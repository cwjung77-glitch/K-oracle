## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best colors for an Autumn Warm individual like Seungmin?
Autumn Warm individuals shine in rich, earthy, and warm-toned colors. Think golden browns, olive greens, moss greens, deep teal, warm reds (like brick red or rust), terracotta, mustard yellow, and warm oranges. Neutrals like camel, warm beige, and deep chocolate brown are also excellent choices.

### What colors should Autumn Warm individuals avoid?
Autumn Warm individuals generally look less vibrant in cool, icy, or overly bright colors that lack warmth. This includes stark black, pure white, cool-toned pastels (like baby pink or lavender with a blue base), icy blues, and true silver. These colors can make the skin appear sallow or washed out.

### Does Seungmin have warm or cool undertones?
Seungmin has distinct **warm undertones**. This is evident in his skin, which often has a golden or peachy glow, and his eyes, which tend to have warm flecks. This warmth is the defining characteristic of his Autumn Warm personal color.
