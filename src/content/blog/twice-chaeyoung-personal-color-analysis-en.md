## Frequently Asked Questions

### What exactly does Spring Warm (봄 웜톤) mean in Personal Color?
Spring Warm refers to a personal color palette characterized by bright, clear, and warm tones. Individuals with this coloring have a warm undertone to their skin, often light to medium hair and eyes, and a naturally fresh, youthful appearance. Their best colors are vibrant and light, like those found in a blossoming spring garden: peach, coral, light yellow, mint green, and warm beige.

### Can a Spring Warm person like Chaeyoung wear black?
While pure, stark black is generally not ideal for a Spring Warm as it can be too harsh and draining, it can be approached strategically. Instead of solid black near the face, opt for black with warm elements, like a black top with gold embroidery, or pair it with warmer accessories. Dark brown, charcoal gray with a warm undertone, or deep olive green can also serve as better alternatives for darker shades, providing depth without overwhelming the Spring Warm's natural radiance.

### What hair colors should a Spring Warm person avoid?
Spring Warm individuals should generally avoid cool-toned hair colors such as ash blonde, ash brown, platinum blonde, or stark, cool-toned black. These shades can create a harsh contrast, making the skin appear sallow or dull and diminishing the natural warmth and brightness that is characteristic of a Spring Warm. Instead, opt for warm, luminous shades like honey blonde, golden brown, strawberry blonde, or light copper.

It also guides your choices in hair color and even fashion, ensuring that every element of your styling works together to enhance your natural beauty, just like it does for K-Pop idols like Chaeyoung!
