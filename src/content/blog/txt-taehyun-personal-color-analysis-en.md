## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Spring Warm Personal Color?
Spring Warm is one of the four main Personal Color seasons, characterized by a **warm undertone**, **high saturation**, and **clear brightness**. Individuals with this coloring often have bright eyes, warm-toned skin (which can range from fair to medium), and hair that naturally has golden or reddish reflections. They thrive in vibrant, fresh, and clear warm colors that mimic the lively hues of springtime.

### Can Spring Warm wear black?
Generally, pure black is too harsh for Spring Warm individuals as it can drain their natural radiance and create a stark contrast that overwhelms their features. Instead, we recommend opting for **soft navy, charcoal gray, dark brown, or olive green** for darker shades. If black must be worn, use it as an accent far from the face, or choose black pieces with a warm-toned pattern or texture.

### What makeup look is best for Spring Warm?
The ideal makeup look for a Spring Warm individual is **fresh, dewy, and vibrant**. Focus on creating a luminous base, using peachy or coral tones for blush and lips, and incorporating warm, shimmery eyeshadows (like champagne, gold, or light peach) to brighten the eyes. Avoid heavy contouring, matte finishes that can look flat, and overly cool-toned or dark makeup, as these can diminish the natural youthful glow.

### Does Taehyun always follow his Personal Color?
While Taehyun undeniably looks his best in Spring Warm tones, like all K-Pop idols, his styling will often vary to fit different comeback concepts, music video themes, and fashion editorials. Stylists might experiment with colors outside his optimal range for artistic expression. However, you'll notice that even in these instances, the overall makeup or styling might be adjusted to soften the impact of less harmonious colors, or the concept itself might lean into a more dramatic or unconventional aesthetic.
