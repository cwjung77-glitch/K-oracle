## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

Legendary actress Song Hye-kyo is the ultimate representation of a **Summer Cool Muted (여름 쿨톤 뮤트)**. Her timeless, elegant, and atmospheric aura is perfectly enhanced by soft, grayish, and cool-toned pastels like dusty rose, ash blue, and muted lavender. When she wears highly saturated or heavy warm tones, it can look unnatural, but in Summer Muted colors, she radiates a breathtaking, natural grace.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

- **The Best (Summer Cool Muted):** Think of her styling in dramas like *The Glory* or *Descendants of the Sun*, where she wore soft, low-contrast, grayish tones. With dusty pink lipsticks, ash-toned hair, and muted clothing, her skin looks incredibly clear, and her features gain a cinematic depth.
- **The Worst (Bright Spring / Deep Autumn):** When styled with neon orange lips, bright yellow clothing, or heavily bronzed makeup, her natural elegance is overpowered. High saturation clashes with the delicate, watercolor-like nature of her skin tone.

## The Secret to the "Goddess" Aesthetic

Summer Cool Muted individuals like Song Hye-kyo possess an inherently sophisticated and calm energy. The key to this personal color is **"Low Contrast and Softness" (부드러움)**. 
Instead of harsh eyeliners and vibrant lip glosses, her makeup artists focus on seamless blending, MLBB (My Lips But Better) colors, and matte or semi-matte skin finishes. This low-contrast approach prevents the makeup from stealing attention away from her flawlessly balanced facial features.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can a Summer Muted person wear dark colors?
Yes, but they should avoid stark, pure Black. Instead, charcoal, slate gray, or deep navy are far more flattering and won't wash out the complexion like a harsh black would.

### Q2: Why does she look so good in minimal makeup?
Summer Muted tones thrive on subtlety. Heavy, vibrant makeup creates too much contrast on their face, masking their natural atmospheric beauty. Minimal, blended makeup perfectly matches the "faded watercolor" aesthetic of the Summer Muted palette.
