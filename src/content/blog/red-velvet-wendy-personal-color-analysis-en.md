## TL;DR (Quick Answer)
* **Official K-Oracle Personal Color:** Summer Cool (Summer Light / Summer Mute).
* **Signature Aesthetic:** Ethereal watercolor lavender, dusty rose-pink blush, misty ash-blonde hair, and translucent, glowing skin.
* **Colors to Embrace:** Soft baby pinks, periwinkle blue, cool mauve, powder blue, and misty gray-lavender.
* **Colors to Avoid:** Heavy mustard yellows, muddy terracotta browns, stark jet-black, and neon orange-reds that overwhelm her delicate, cool undertones.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling
To understand Wendy's personal color, we have to look at how different palettes interact with her natural complexion. As a quintessential **Summer Cool**, Wendy possesses a delicate, clear skin clarity with cool pink undertones. 

When Wendy steps out in her optimal **Summer Light** palette—think misty lavender knits, soft dusty rose blushes, and platinum ash-blonde locks—her skin literally seems to light up from within. Her facial features appear softer, her dark circles visually vanish, and her overall vibe radiates a dreamy, effortless elegance. The harmony between her natural coloring and cool-toned pastels creates a seamless, high-fashion finish.

On the flip side, when she is styled in high-contrast, heavy, warm tones—such as a stark mustard yellow or a heavy brick-red lipstick—a visual clash occurs. Warm, earthy pigments pull the color out of her face, making her skin look slightly sallow and drawing unwanted attention to texture and shadows. Similarly, jet-black hair can sometimes look a bit too harsh against her delicate face, whereas soft ash-browns and cool platinum highlights make her features pop with softness.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can Wendy ever wear warm colors?**
While her optimal palette strictly belongs to Summer Cool, K-Pop styling often requires versatility. If Wendy wears a warm-toned outfit for a specific concept, her makeup artists typically neutralize it by keeping her base makeup impeccably cool, utilizing blue-based pink lips and heavy cool-toned eye framing to balance the overall look.

**What is the difference between Summer Light and Summer Mute?**
Both fall under the broader Summer Cool umbrella in our database. Summer Light leans toward airy, pastel, high-brightness shades (like baby pink and sky blue), while Summer Mute leans toward soft, dusty, desaturated tones (like mauve, dusty rose, and cocoa-infused gray). Wendy effortlessly transitions between both depending on the season and album concept, though her brightest moments shine in high-clarity Summer Light pastels.

**How do I know if I share Wendy's personal color?**
If you look best in silver jewelry over gold, feel refreshed in pastel pinks and blues rather than orange and mustard, and notice that your skin glows under cool-toned, soft lighting, you might just share Wendy's stunning Summer Cool palette!
