## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Autumn Warm types wear black?
Yes, but with care. Autumn Warm types can wear black, but it often looks best when it's a softer black, charcoal, or paired with warmer accessories and makeup near the face. Avoid stark, true black directly against the skin as it can sometimes appear too harsh and drain color. Opt for deep espresso brown or olive green as alternatives.

### What jewelry colors suit Autumn Warm?
Gold, rose gold, bronze, and copper are the most flattering metals for Autumn Warm individuals. These warm-toned metals beautifully complement their skin's undertones, making them glow. Silver or platinum can appear too cool and stark.

### How does Autumn Warm differ from Spring Warm?
While both are warm tones, Autumn Warm is characterized by deeper, richer, and more muted earthy tones, reflecting the colors of a late autumn landscape. Spring Warm, on the other hand, is brighter, clearer, and more vibrant, reminiscent of fresh spring blooms and sunshine. Autumn Warm has more depth and a slightly softer quality compared to Spring Warm's lively clarity.

### What K-Beauty trends are perfect for Autumn Warm?
Autumn Warm individuals can perfectly embrace trends like "Muted Coral" or "Brick Red" lips, "Bronze Goddess" makeup looks, "Cozy Brown" or "Auburn" hair colors, and "Latte Makeup" which emphasizes warm, monochrome brown tones. Any trend focusing on natural, earthy, and warm glow will be a hit.

### Can Hyein pull off bright colors?
Generally, pure, vibrant brights (especially cool-toned ones) are not ideal for Autumn Warm types as they can overpower their natural warmth. However, Hyein can wear *deep, rich versions* of brights that lean warm, such as a deep pumpkin orange, a rich olive green, or a muted mustard yellow. The key is that the color retains a certain level of depth and warmth, rather than being overtly neon or icy.
