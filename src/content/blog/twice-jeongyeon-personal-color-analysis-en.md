## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Spring Warm tones wear red?
Absolutely! But the key is to choose the *right* red. Spring Warm individuals like Jeongyeon shine in clear, bright, warm reds such as tomato red, poppy red, or a vibrant coral red. Avoid deep, cool-toned reds like burgundy or true ruby, which can be too heavy and clash with their light, clear warmth.

### What kind of accessories suit Spring Warm individuals?
For Spring Warm, gold jewelry is your best friend! The warm metallic tones beautifully complement the golden undertones in the skin. Pearls with a creamy or yellowish tint, as well as stones in clear, warm colors like amber, peridot, clear quartz, or light aquamarine, are also excellent choices. Avoid stark silver or dark, heavy jewelry.

### Are there any specific K-Beauty brands or product lines good for Spring Warm?
Many K-Beauty brands offer fantastic options for Spring Warm! Look for product lines that emphasize "fresh," "bright," "coral," "peach," or "warm tone" in their descriptions. Brands like Rom&nd, Peripera, Etude House, and AMUSE often have extensive ranges of warm-toned lip tints, blushes, and eyeshadows that are perfect. Always swatch products to ensure they have that clear, warm undertone.

### How does Spring Warm differ from Autumn Warm?
While both are warm tones, they have distinct characteristics. Spring Warm (like Jeongyeon) is characterized by **brightness, clarity, and lightness**, evoking the fresh, vibrant energy of spring flowers. Their colors are clear and often pastel-like or vibrant. Autumn Warm, on the other hand, is defined by **depth, richness, and muted earthiness**, reminiscent of autumn leaves. Their colors are deeper, more subdued, and often more saturated.

### Can Jeongyeon (or any Spring Warm) wear black?
While black isn't in the ideal Spring Warm palette, it's a versatile color! For Jeongyeon, it's best worn strategically. If she wears black, it should be as an accent, in a fabric with some sheen or texture, or paired with a warm-toned accessory (like a gold necklace) or warm makeup (e.g., a peachy blush and lip). Avoid wearing solid black directly around the face without any warmth to balance it out. A deep warm brown or charcoal can often be a softer, more harmonious alternative to stark black.
