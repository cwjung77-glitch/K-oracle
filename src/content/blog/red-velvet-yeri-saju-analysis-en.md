Yu Rooster (Metal) and Myo Rabbit (Wood) sit directly opposite each other. Metal cuts Wood to shape it into something useful and beautiful.

* It explains her ability to pivot seamlessly between singing, song writing, variety shows, and serious dramatic acting roles.
* It bestows a quick wit and high adaptability under pressure.

In her month pillar, **Jeong Fire** acts as the *Pyeon-Gwan* (Seven Killings or Passionate Authority star). Fire tempers Metal to craft a masterpiece. This placement explains why Yeri shines brightest when challenged. The intense spotlight of the public eye does not intimidate her; rather, it refines her talent and elevates her status.
