## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

Blackpink's Jisoo is a classic **True Autumn (가을 웜톤 트루)**. Her elegant, luxurious, and "classic actress" aura is perfectly enhanced by deep, rich, and warm tones like muted brick red, dark olive, and espresso brown. When she wears cool or neon tones, it clashes with her natural warmth, but in Autumn colors, she radiates a regal, timeless beauty.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

- **The Best (Autumn Warm):** Think of her iconic Dior campaigns wearing rich burgundy or deep brown. Her skin looks flawless, and her features become incredibly distinct and luxurious.
- **The Worst (Bright Cool):** When styled in icy platinum blonde hair or neon pink lipstick, her natural elegance is overpowered, and her skin tone appears slightly pale or washed out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Jisoo a Spring Warm or Autumn Warm?
While she can pull off some darker Spring colors, her absolute best palette is True Autumn. She looks most striking in deeper, richer tones rather than bright, pastel Spring colors.

### Q2: What is her signature makeup look?
Her signature look involves soft, matte skin, minimal eye makeup with a focus on warm brown shading, and a blurred brick-red or muted rose lip.
