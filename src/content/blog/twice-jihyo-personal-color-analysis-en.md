## TL;DR (Quick Answer)
TWICE’s powerhouse leader Jihyo is the ultimate embodiment of the **Autumn Warm (True/Deep Autumn)** palette. Blessed with a rich, honeyed skin undertone and an athletic, golden-bronze radiance, she commands attention not in icy pastels or neon brights, but in saturated, earthy luxury. From molten copper hair to terracotta lips and espresso velvet tailoring, Jihyo’s signature aesthetic marries high-octane stage charisma with the sophisticated warmth of the golden hour.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

### The Power Palette: Autumn Warm Excellence
When Jihyo steps out in the rich, pigment-heavy spectrum of the Autumn Warm palette, her skin achieves a seamless, lit-from-within luminosity. 
* **Red-Carpet Royalty:** Think of her in deep olive green silk gowns, rich burnt orange halter dresses, or structured caramel leather. These hues amplify the natural golden undertones of her skin, making her look expensive, grounded, and impossibly radiant.
* **Music Video Mastery:** In recent solo eras, her transition into warm copper hair paired with bronze-monochrome makeup created an iconic visual harmony. The color doesn't compete with her features; it frames her expressive eyes and sculpts her high cheekbones.
* **Airport Chic:** Oversized camel trench coats, tortoiseshell accessories, and warm beige knitwear project an effortless, old-money luxury that highlights her athletic silhouette.

### The Contrast Clash: What Drains Her
Because Jihyo’s underlying pigment is deeply warm and golden, cool-toned or stark colors instantly disrupt her natural harmony.
* **The Ice Trap:** Icy pastels—such as lavender, baby blue, or mint green—wash out her natural warmth, casting an ashen, tired shadow over her complexion. 
* **The Neon Warning:** High-intensity neon pinks or electric blues hijack her face, drawing attention away from her features and creating a jarring dissonance with her skin’s golden-bronze glow.
* **Jet Black Mistake:** While jet black hair can sometimes work with high-contrast styling, it often hardens her features. A softer espresso or rich dark chocolate is infinitely more flattering than stark, flat black.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is TWICE Jihyo (Park Jihyo) classified as an Autumn Warm?
Jihyo possesses a naturally golden, medium-tan skin depth that holds warmth effortlessly. Unlike Winter types who thrive on high contrast and cool undertones, or Spring types who lean into bright clarity, Jihyo's aesthetic is anchored by depth, richness, and earthiness. Her skin responds best to pigments containing yellow and red undertones rather than blue ones, placing her squarely in the Autumn Warm (True/Deep Autumn) category.

### What is their single most iconic red-carpet or music video styling?
Jihyo's solo debut era styling remains the gold standard for her personal color profile. Her hair styled in rich, molten copper waves paired with a bronzed, sun-kissed complexion and a deep terracotta lip created an unforgettable visual narrative. This look perfectly married her athletic, powerhouse stage presence with high-fashion editorial warmth, proving that earthy tones can dominate the global pop stage.

### How can fans with a similar skin tone replicate this look daily?
To channel Jihyo's Autumn Warm glow, ditch the cool-toned pink blushes and silver highlighters in favor of golden-champagne highlighters and terracotta cream blushes. For everyday makeup, focus on monochromatic warmth: a wash of burnt orange or bronze on the eyelids, a warm bronzer swept across the cheekbones, and a comfortable satin-finish cinnamon nude lip. Pair these with wardrobe staples in olive, mustard, camel, and rich brown to instantly elevate your natural radiance.
