## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

Actress Han So Hee exudes a dark, captivating, and intensely chic aura that perfectly aligns with a **Deep Winter Cool (겨울 쿨톤 딥)** personal color. She is the epitome of "dark feminine" energy. Her pale skin contrasts sharply with her naturally dark hair and eyes, allowing her to command heavy, deep, and cool colors like charcoal, burgundy, and deep plum that would easily overwhelm softer personal colors.

## Visual Proof: Best vs Worst Styling

Han So Hee's styling is a masterclass in embracing edgy, cool-toned depth.

*   **Best Styling (Winter Cool):** Han So Hee looks her most iconic when styled with high contrast and deep coolness. Black leather, dark burgundy lips, smoky cool-toned eye makeup, and jet-black hair make her look incredibly striking and powerful. She easily carries heavy, intense colors that define the Deep Winter palette.
*   **Worst Styling (Spring Warm):** If she were to be styled in bright, warm, bouncy colors—like neon coral, bright yellow, or warm peach—it would completely clash with her intense, moody aura. Warm, light colors tend to make her look disconnected, stripping away her signature edge.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is the difference between True Winter and Deep Winter?
While True Winters (like Jisoo) look great in clear, vivid cool colors (like true red or royal blue), Deep Winters (like Han So Hee) look best in the *darkest* cool colors. They share boundaries with Deep Autumn, meaning they look incredible in very dark, moody shades like charcoal and blackened burgundy.

### 2. How does Han So Hee pull off the "smoky" look so well?
Deep Winters have the natural depth and contrast to handle heavy, dark eye makeup without looking overwhelmed. The smoky, cool-toned eye enhances her natural intensity.
