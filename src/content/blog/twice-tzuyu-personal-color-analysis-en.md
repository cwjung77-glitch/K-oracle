Welcome back to K-Oracle, your expert for K-Beauty and Personal Color! Today, we're diving deep into the world of one of K-Pop's most captivating icons: TWICE's Tzuyu. Her flawless beauty and elegant demeanor have made her a global idol. But what's the secret behind her consistently perfect radiance? The answer lies in her **Personal Color: Autumn Warm**.

As K-Beauty consultants and Personal Color Analysts, we've meticulously analyzed Tzuyu's features. Her warm skin undertone, the deep color of her eyes and hair, perfectly harmonize with the rich, muted tones of autumn. These colors lend her a natural warmth and elegance that makes her shine. Let's discover together how Tzuyu masterfully utilizes her Autumn Warm palette to optimally showcase her beauty.

## TL;DR (Quick Answer)

TWICE's Tzuyu is a classic **Autumn Warm** type. This means she looks best in warm, muted, and deep colors that reflect the earthy tones of autumn. Think olive green, rust red, mustard yellow, warm brown, and terracotta. Cool, pastel, or very bright and garish tones should be avoided, as they can make her complexion appear sallow. Her makeup should also feature warm undertones to enhance her natural glow.

## Visual Proof: Best vs. Worst Styling

The right color palette can make a dramatic difference. Here are some examples of how Tzuyu, as an Autumn Warm type, appears in her best and less favorable looks:

**Best Styling for Autumn Warm:**

*   **Earthy Tones:** Tzuyu looks stunning in colors like olive green, khaki, warm brown, or rust red. These tones reflect the natural warmth of her complexion and make her skin appear radiant and healthy. An olive green dress or a warm brown blazer accentuates her elegance and depth.
*   **Golden Glow:** Warm gold and bronze tones, both in clothing and makeup, are perfect for her. A look with golden eyeshadow or antique gold accessories highlights her eye color and gives her a luxurious shimmer.
*   **Muted Reds:** A deep brick red or a warm berry red on the lips or as an accent in her clothing harmonizes wonderfully with her skin tone, lending her a mature, sophisticated aura.
*   **Warm Hair:** Hair colors like warm chocolate brown, chestnut brown, or a muted auburn emphasize the warmth of her complexion, making her face appear softer and more vibrant.

**Less Favorable Styling for Autumn Warm:**

*   **Cool Pastels:** Very light, cool pastel colors like icy blue, lavender, or light pink can make Tzuyu's complexion appear sallow and lifeless. These tones clash with her warm undertone, diminishing her natural warmth.
*   **Harsh, Cool Blacks and Whites:** Pure, cool black or stark white can be too harsh for her complexion, casting a shadow on her face. She looks better in off-white, cream, or a warm charcoal gray instead of true black.
*   **Bright, Cool Colors:** Vibrant fuchsia pink or a bright royal blue can also appear too intense and unharmonious, overpowering the muted elegance of the Autumn type.
*   **Ashy Hair Colors:** Hair colors with a strong ashy or platinum blonde undertone can make Tzuyu's face appear harsh and suppress her natural warmth.
